Snowfall

Bring a little winter to your working day.
Brush away snow, build snowmen and enjoy visits from a playful robin.

A snowy desktop

Watch snow settle on windows and the Dock. Let flakes fall in front of windows, behind the active window or on the desktop only.

Take a moment to play

Brush through drifts and watch snow tumble as you move windows. Swirl your mouse to grow snowballs, then pick them up, roll them around and stack them into snowmen.

A visiting robin

Watch him hop, forage, preen and occasionally find a worm. He might chase your pointer, perch on it for a moment or fly off with his latest catch.

Make winter your own

Adjust the snowfall, flake styles, colours, wind and thawing, or choose your own image for the flakes. Mix styles and create the atmosphere you want.

A little character

Build snowmen and arrange their accessories. The robin can explore windows, the Dock and snowman decorations, adding little surprises to your day.

Close at hand

Open Settings, pause the snow or reset everything from the menu bar. Snowfall is installed on your Mac simply as Snowfall.

Optional desktop effects

Screen-recording permission enables snow on desktop icons, snow settling on top of the Dock and additional robin interactions. Screen images are processed locally on your Mac and are not saved or shared.
