Orbit: Pixel Inspector

See every pixel. A live Retina loupe, exact colour values and a pixel ruler that follow your pointer.

View on the Mac App StoreGetting started →

For macOS · No accounts, ads or tracking

Inspect every detail

Magnify the actual pixels around your pointer. Use the pixel grid and adjustable zoom to inspect edges, alignment and small details on a Retina display.

Read exact colours

See the sRGB colour of the pixel under your pointer in hex and RGB. The colour chip follows your pointer alongside the loupe.

Measure in pixels

A ruler uses the sampled colour and measures in device pixels, giving you another way to check the detail on screen.

Point, inspect, carry on.

Orbit reads screen pixels only to show them to you. Nothing is recorded, saved to disk or uploaded.
