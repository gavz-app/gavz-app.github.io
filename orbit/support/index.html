Orbit: Pixel Inspector

Getting started & support

Point at a pixel. Orbit brings the detail closer.

Allow screen access

When you first launch Orbit, macOS asks for Screen & System Audio Recording permission. Orbit needs this to read the pixels near your pointer and show them in the loupe. It does not record audio or save screen captures.

Allow Orbit under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, then quit and reopen the app if macOS asks you to.

Using Orbit

Move your pointer to inspect the magnified pixels, colour and ruler. Click the Dock icon or press Control–Option–Command–Space to show or hide Orbit. You can change that global shortcut in Orbit → Settings.

  • Control–Option–Command–G: toggle the pixel grid.
  • Control–Option–Command–H: show the shortcuts overlay.
  • Control–Option–Command–+ or −: adjust zoom.
  • Control–Option–Command–0: reset size and zoom.

Need a hand?

Email support@gavz.app with your Orbit version, macOS version and a short description of the problem. Avoid including private screen content in any screenshots you share.
