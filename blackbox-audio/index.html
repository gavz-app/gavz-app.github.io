Coming to the Mac App Store · Version 2026.1 · US$4.99 at launch

Convert your collection

Create MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC/M4A and other formats supported by macOS. Run more than one Convert action to make multiple outputs from the same source.

Shape the sound

Normalize loudness, adjust volume, change speed with or without preserving pitch, trim silence or duration, and add fades.

Listen before saving

Switch between your original and processed preview. Set a listening range, loop it, and hear the changes before transforming the batch.

See your audio

Compare waveforms and loudness. Explore detected tempo, key, instrument activity and musical parts. Music analysis provides estimates that depend on the recording.

Make metadata consistent

Copy all tags, remove them, or choose what to keep. Apply fixed values to a batch and optionally embed artwork from a separate image file.

Repeat your workflow

Save profiles that remember your changes. Run them with Shortcuts, AppleScript or the command line. Save beside originals or choose a folder and preserve its structure.

Local processing. New files.

Your audio stays on your Mac. BlackBox writes new output files, leaves your originals intact, and gives existing filenames a unique name.
